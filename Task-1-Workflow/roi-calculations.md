# Task 1 — ROI and measurement worksheet

**Process:** Student document request, with the Principal's signature identified by the candidate as the main delay.
**Evidence status:** No measured wait times, request volume, repeat trips, or staff effort are available. Do not treat the example below as ICFAI data.

## Measure these separately

| Measure | Formula | Current evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Average signature wait | Total elapsed time from submission for signature to obtaining signature ÷ requests observed | Not measured |
| Repeat-trip rate | Requests requiring an extra trip because the signature was not ready ÷ requests observed | Not measured |
| Staff time saved per month | Monthly volume × (current staff minutes/request − proposed staff minutes/request) − maintenance minutes | Not measured |
| Student trips avoided per month | Monthly volume × (current average repeat trips − proposed average repeat trips) | Not measured |

Do not count elapsed waiting as staff labour saved. A student waiting for a signature and an administrator spending time processing a request are different measures.

## Inputs to collect with permission

- Number of document requests in a clearly defined period (or a justified range).
- Submission time and time signature becomes available, for a small anonymised sample.
- Number of extra visits caused specifically by signature unavailability.
- Staff minutes spent handling each request and answering status enquiries.
- Proposed tracker/register maintenance time.
- One-time setup effort and any ongoing cost.

## Staff-time formulas

- Gross monthly staff minutes saved = `monthly volume × (current staff minutes/request − proposed staff minutes/request)`
- Net monthly staff minutes saved = `gross minutes saved − monthly maintenance minutes`
- Net monthly staff hours saved = `net minutes ÷ 60`
- Break-even months = `one-time implementation minutes ÷ net monthly minutes saved`, only if net monthly savings are positive.

## Illustrative example only

The following worked example uses arbitrary assumptions only:

| Input / result | Illustrative value | Calculation |
|---|---:|---|
| Requests per month | 100 | Assumed for demonstration |
| Current staff effort per request | 20 minutes | Assumed; not measured |
| Proposed staff effort per request | 10 minutes | Assumed; not measured |
| Gross staff time saved | 1,000 minutes/month | `100 × (20 − 10)` |
| Tracker maintenance | 120 minutes/month | Assumed; not measured |
| Net staff time saved | 880 minutes/month (14.67 hours) | `1,000 − 120`; then `880 ÷ 60` |
| One-time setup effort | 1,200 minutes | Assumed; not measured |
| Illustrative break-even | 1.36 months | `1,200 ÷ 880`, if net savings recur monthly |

These values are arbitrary assumptions used to demonstrate the arithmetic. They are **not measured at ICFAI**, do not estimate the Principal-signature wait, and must not be presented as evidence of actual savings. The calculation only estimates staff effort under the stated assumptions; it does not convert student waiting time into staff savings.

For a real pilot, replace the example with measured or defensibly estimated inputs. If reliable inputs are unavailable, keep the formulas and mark the inputs unknown rather than claiming a numerical benefit.
