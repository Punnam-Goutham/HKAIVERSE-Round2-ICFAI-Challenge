## Key proposal

**Task 1 working choice:** Student document-request workflow. The candidate reported on 23 October 2026 that the current sequence is: visit the Student Department, write a request letter, obtain the Principal's signature, return to the Student Department for signature verification, and receive the document. The candidate identified obtaining the Principal's signature as the main delay. This is candidate-reported, not independently verified; no wait times or request volumes have been measured. The proposal keeps the Principal's signature and Student Department verification mandatory, and suggests a simple status register / approved routing window to reduce uncertainty. No independent interview or direct observation is claimed.

**Task 2 recommendation:** Keep foundational CS and explicitly preserve Data Science, Machine Learning, Blockchain Technology and Power Skills I–IV. Streamline, without deleting mandatory outcomes, Essence of Indian Traditional Knowledge, Environmental Sciences, Dynamics of Social Change and Constitution of India. The candidate reports that Integral Transforms and Probability & Statistics and Fundamentals of Signal Processing have already been reduced for junior batches; this has not been independently verified. Add credit-neutral production-engineering, secure-development and AI-assisted engineering practice, and an integrated capstone. A specific 38-credit existing-course assessment-update plan is documented: 6 credits in Semester III, 8 in Semester IV, 10 in Semester V, and 14 in Semester VI. Using the candidate-reported total of 168 credits, 38 affected credits equals 22.62% (about 22.6%). This is within the 20–25% band if 168 is the correct official total; that total has not been independently verified.

## Evidence and limitations

- Official facts must link to an official source and identify the date accessed.
- Field observations must be real, permission-based, and dated. Record roles only; do not collect names, IDs, marks, fees, signatures, credentials, or private messages.
- Synthetic test cases are test designs, not field observations.
- Calculations must identify measured inputs, estimates, and assumptions separately.
- AI exercise outputs must be saved from actual runs and independently checked. Do not invent model outputs, errors, or validation.
- Faculty/alumni validation is pending unless a real willing participant gives feedback.
- Public job postings can change or expire; save a dated copy or notes and confirm the live posting before final submission.
- Do not include confidential college information or personal data in a public repository.
